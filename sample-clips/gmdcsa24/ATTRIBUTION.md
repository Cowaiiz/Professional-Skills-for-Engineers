# GMDCSA-24 sample clips

These two unmodified video files and their descriptions come from Ekram Alam's
[GMDCSA-24 dataset, version 2.1 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13354453).
The [dataset record](https://zenodo.org/records/13354453) identifies the license
as [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- `Subject 1/ADL/13.mp4` shows walking and sitting.
- `Subject 4/Fall/07.mp4` shows a staged fall.
